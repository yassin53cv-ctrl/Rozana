About Rozana

Rozana began with a simple idea: a place where the day slows down. Coffee and cold drinks made with care in the café, and a restaurant menu cooked fresh, grilled over flame and baked until golden.

Every plate is prepared with good ingredients and finished by hand, served in a warm, elegant room that feels like an invitation.
